v0.0.19 Dev Log - Suitor Arc
First Dates: Sir Aldren Thornwild & Lady Vaela Rhyss
New focus: the suitor selection finally moves from ceremony to courtship, with the first two one-on-one dates

Caelan meets two very different suitors back to back, with Serelyne's handmaidens watching and a report to give afterward

Key beats:

A short bridge after the ceremony: Caelan names Aldren as the first suitor, Serelyne adds Vaela for the same day, and the dates begin the next morning when you sleep

Morning preparation: Veletia's new transitional tunic-dress, Maera braiding Caelan's hair, and Serelyne's three rules (observe, listen, respond honestly) before the dates begin

Aldren Thornwild, the garden walk: a white rose from Edran, a conversation about parents and what a sword is for, and the first person to simply ask Caelan "does it hurt?"

Stone-skipping on the lake with Aldren, the throwing arm that no longer works the way it did, and a quiet kiss on the knuckles at the garden gate

Choice after Aldren: his kiss can leave Caelan's heart racing, read as a kind gesture and nothing more, or feel too forward

Vaela Rhyss, the Iron Garden: no tea and no small talk, a practice rapier thrown across the yard, and four sparring bouts in the Dawncourt Blade Form, which uses the hips as the engine of the strike

Caelan lands a touch on Vaela in the fourth bout, and the fight turns into a near-kiss that she ends on her own terms ("I want you to choose me, not fall into me")

Choice after Vaela: she can terrify and attract Caelan, come across as skilled but too aggressive, or leave Caelan feeling nothing

Serelyne's chambers: the handmaidens' report, the question of which touch lingered, a look at her four-axis scoring (physical, emotional, intellectual, political), and her observation that Aldren assumed fragility where Vaela assumed strength

Outcome:

first use of suitor affection tracking: $aldren_affection, $vaela_affection and $lesbian_curiosity are set by your choices and carry forward into later suitor content

Aldren is the steady, patient romance and Vaela is the fierce, challenging one, with neither presented as the better choice

Maera, Torin and Edran have new post-date lines in their random conversations, so the dates are noticed around the castle

the first dates take the place of a night's sleep: the day counter and the daily riddle and ingredient limits still reset as normal

closes with Caelan alone in the corridor, a white rose in hand and two different touches lingering, ahead of the remaining suitors
