v0.0.18 Dev Log — Valetia Arc
Sessions 6–7: Foundation Dressing
New focus: the visible layers of a queen's wardrobe, following on from Sessions 1–5, which only ever covered underclothes

Veletia moves Caelan from what's hidden beneath the gown to what the court actually sees

Key beats:

Session 6 opens with a summons to the Bloom Room and a full four-layer build: chemise, corset, bodice, gown, each one explained in turn as Veletia dresses her

Corset fitting is the emotional hinge of the session, branches on how Caelan reacts to seeing her own silhouette (fascinated, unsettled, or wanting to move past it) before the paths rejoin

Bodice and gown layered on top, culminating in the first look in the mirror as a fully dressed woman rather than a prince in borrowed clothes

Dressing mechanics taught explicitly: the full eight-minute routine broken into timed steps, then drilled solo until she can do all four layers unassisted (eleven minutes, several things crooked, but done)

Session ends with Veletia gifting a small brooch and a walk back to her chamber in the gown alone, tested against a servant's reaction in the corridor

Session 7 opens in a converted gallery, three skirt lengths (tea, ankle, court train) used to teach walking, turning, sitting, rising

Curtsey taught in four depths (shallow, medium, deep, deep with train), tied explicitly back to the drop-step from her combat training, same core mechanics, different room

Stair work added last, ascending and descending in each skirt length, capped by managing a train on the stairs without stepping on it

Final assessment is a single unbroken circuit, walk, sit, rise, curtsey, stairs, turn, mirror, performed in front of both Veletia and Maera with no corrections allowed

Outcome:

closes out the dressing arc's foundation phase before the next block (color, jewelry, cosmetics, dance)

reinforces the throughline that combat training and court training are the same underlying skillset (core, hips, breath) aimed at two different rooms

$valetia_trust and $femininity_style both see their largest single-session gains of the arc so far
