🟊 Prince Caelan Velmoria Age: 20 Role: Protagonist, Heir to the Throne Gender: Male (transitioning to Female — Player-determined) Personality: Determined, intelligent, and conflicted. Caelan has been raised as a prince but possesses a quiet sensitivity often frowned upon in Velmoria’s warrior culture. He is brave but internally divided as he faces pressure to abandon his identity and reshape it entirely. His character is shaped by player choices—he may become a proud and radiant queen, a bitter and reluctant ruler, or something unique in between. 
👑 Queen Alinora Velmoria Age: 42 Role: Reigning Monarch of Velmoria, Mother of Caelan Personality: Regal, pragmatic, and emotionally reserved. Alinora embodies centuries of royal blood and bears the weight of Velmoria’s legacy with grace and steel. While she loves her son deeply, her devotion to the crown is absolute. She believes in survival through tradition and sees Caelan’s transformation as not only necessary but noble. To her, personal identity is secondary to duty.
